# Handling repeated account lockouts

Repeated sign-in attempts can extend a lockout and may indicate a stale device session or suspicious activity. Unlocking an account is a privileged change and requires identity verification by authorized identity support.

1. Ask the requester to stop repeated sign-in attempts and note the approximate time and displayed message.
2. Confirm which approved service is affected and whether the requester recently changed their password.
3. Do not collect credentials or MFA codes; direct the requester to the approved account-recovery channel.
4. Route the case to authorized identity support to verify the requester and review sign-in activity before an unlock.
5. After an authorized unlock, ask the requester to update saved credentials only through approved device settings.

Escalate to the security team if the requester does not recognize the activity. Do not unlock an account solely because a ticket asks for it.
