# Multi-factor enrollment assistance

Use the approved identity portal for multi-factor enrollment. Enrollment changes account security settings, so support staff must verify identity and follow the organization's approval procedure before changing enrollment.

1. Confirm the requester can reach the known identity portal and record the non-sensitive error text.
2. Check whether the account is already enrolled using the approved support view; do not ask for a code or device passcode.
3. If a device was replaced or enrollment must be reset, route the request to authorized identity support for verification.
4. Have the requester complete enrollment through the official portal after the authorized change.
5. Record whether enrollment succeeded, without recording recovery codes or authentication secrets.

Unexpected enrollment prompts or unrecognized devices should be handled as a security concern and reviewed by the security team.
