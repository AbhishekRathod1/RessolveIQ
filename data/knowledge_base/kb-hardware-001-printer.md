# Basic printer-error checks

These non-invasive checks apply to a managed office printer. Do not open electrical panels or attempt repairs that require a technician.

1. Record the printer's asset label, displayed error, and whether nearby users see the same issue.
2. Check that the paper tray is seated and contains the correct paper size for the requested job.
3. If the device visibly reports a paper jam, follow its posted user-safe instructions; do not force a stuck mechanism.
4. Confirm the printer is awake and connected using its normal status display.
5. If the error continues, stop retries and send the error text and asset label to the printer support team.

For smoke, unusual heat, or damaged wiring, stop using the device and contact facilities through the approved urgent channel.
