# Low-storage warnings on managed computers

A low-storage warning can affect updates and application performance. User files may be business records, so support must not delete data without the owner's review.

1. Record the warning text, device asset label, and time it appeared.
2. Ask whether the warning prevents a specific approved work task.
3. Have the requester review only their own known temporary items through normal settings; do not direct deletion of unfamiliar folders.
4. If space remains low, route the case to desktop support for an approved storage review.
5. Confirm any cleanup plan with the user and follow retention policy before removing managed or shared data.

Do not use cleanup scripts or commands from unverified sources. Escalate if the warning is accompanied by unexpected encryption or security alerts.
