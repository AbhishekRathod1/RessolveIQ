# Office Wi-Fi connection checks

Use the managed wireless network and approved device settings. Do not share wireless passwords in a ticket or switch to an unknown access point.

1. Confirm the device is in the expected office location and identify the managed Wi-Fi network by its published name.
2. Check whether another approved service works and whether nearby users have a similar connection problem.
3. If the device reports that it is disconnected, use its normal network menu to reconnect to the known managed network.
4. Record the approximate time, location, device asset label, and any non-sensitive error shown.
5. Escalate repeated drops or a multi-user outage to network support for authorized access-point review.

Do not bypass network authentication or disclose a shared credential to troubleshoot connectivity.
