# Reviewing a role-assignment request

Role assignments can expose sensitive information or administrative functions. A ticket is not itself approval to change a role.

1. Record the requested application, role name, requester account, and business task.
2. Confirm the application owner and required approval route from the published access catalog.
3. Ask the owner to approve the narrowest role that enables the requested task.
4. Route an approved request to authorized identity support; do not change the role directly from an unverified message.
5. Have the requester and owner verify the result, then record the approval reference and outcome.

If the requested role is privileged or the approver is unclear, stop and require additional human review.
