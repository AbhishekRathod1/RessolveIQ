# Safe password-reset requests

Password resets change account credentials and must be handled through the approved identity process by an authorized person. This article is general guidance, not proof of identity. Support staff must not request, record, or repeat a password, recovery code, or MFA code.

1. Confirm the affected account identifier and the exact sign-in message without collecting a password or one-time code.
2. Direct the requester to the organization's approved self-service recovery portal using a known internal bookmark.
3. If self-service recovery fails, route the ticket to authorized identity support for the required identity verification.
4. Have authorized staff perform any reset only after verification and follow the organization's account-change policy.
5. Ask the requester to sign in through the normal approved flow and record only the outcome, never the new credential.

Do not bypass identity checks or send temporary credentials through chat or email. Require human review before any account change.
